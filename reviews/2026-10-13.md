## Ad hoc — worth knowing (link from Luke, not yet harvested)

**ArtCraft "Crafting Apps"**, https://github.com/storytold, the "open-sourced Adobe tools" post.
- **What it actually is:** these are **not from Adobe**. They're clean-room Rust rebuilds of Adobe and Microsoft apps by ArtCraft (GitHub org `storytold`, the team behind the FakeYou voice site): PhotoCraft (Photoshop, reads PSD), LightCraft (Lightroom), FilmCraft (Premiere), EffectCraft (After Effects), VectorCraft (Illustrator), DesignCraft (InDesign), PdfCraft (Acrobat), plus Word, Excel, PowerPoint, AutoCAD and Pro Tools equivalents.
- **Verified (GitHub API, 2026-10-13):** Apache-2.0. Most app repos were created 30 Sep–1 Oct 2026, so they're two weeks old. PhotoCraft has 53,785 stars, 8,976 forks and 1,371 open issues, with releases v0.6.0 to v0.8.2 shipped 10–12 Oct. Its README says "early alpha". The size of the fork and issue counts points to real attention rather than bought stars.
- **Takeaway:** it's interesting to watch, but it doesn't replace Creative Cloud for client work yet. It's alpha, it's two weeks old, and its fidelity to real PSD/Premiere project files is unproven. Its binaries come from a brand-new org, so only try it on a spare machine. Revisit in about 3 months; if PhotoCraft or FilmCraft hold up, they could cut licence costs for occasional editors.
- **Relevant to:** the social/video team (FilmCraft, EffectCraft) and design (PhotoCraft, VectorCraft). Not a Claude Code change, so nothing goes in the manifest.
