# Re-sort — 2026-10-13

Not a new harvest. Every **Rejected** row across the 18 earlier reviews was
re-read (97 rows) because one verdict was doing two jobs: "don't install this"
and "this isn't worth knowing". Those are different calls, and useful
information was being thrown away with the junk.

**18 posts move to Worth knowing.** The rest stay Rejected, and most of them
deserve it — legal and platform-ToS risk, fake features, SkillSpector

appears anywhere in the caption, audio or frames.

## What stays Rejected, and why the distinction matters

79 of the 97 rows stay put. The reasons cluster:

- **Legal or reputational** — contact scraping and cold outreach, engagement
  automation against client accounts, AI-disclosure defeat, guardrail removal,
  mass account creation. These are the ones where a verified, working tool is
  still the wrong answer.
- **Fake or unverifiable** — a Claude "focus mode" that doesn't exist; posts
  whose payload never appears in caption, audio or frames.
- **Scanner findings** — two skills scored DO-NOT-INSTALL by SkillSpector.
- **Ads and funnels** — sponsored posts, affiliate pitches, vendors ranking
  their own competitors, prompts gated behind a lead magnet.
- **Already covered** — hosted Supabase, Vercel TLS, built-in `WebSearch`.

Worth knowing is not a softer Rejected. Nothing risky or fake was moved into it.
